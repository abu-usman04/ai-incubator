# Acme Cloud — Product FAQ

## What does Acme Cloud offer?
Acme Cloud provides three products: **Vector DB** (managed vector search), **Pipeline** (managed
data ingestion), and **Insight** (a dashboard and alerting layer). They can be used independently
or together.

## Pricing
- **Starter**: free, up to 1 project and 100,000 vectors.
- **Team**: $49 per month, up to 10 projects and 5 million vectors, email support.
- **Business**: $199 per month, unlimited projects, 50 million vectors, priority support and a
  99.9% uptime SLA.
Annual billing receives a 20% discount.

## Refund policy
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and are not refundable for the current period. Annual
plans are eligible for a prorated refund within the first 30 days. To request a refund, contact
billing@acmecloud.example.

## Supported regions
Data can be hosted in Frankfurt (eu-central), Singapore (ap-southeast), or Virginia (us-east).
Region is chosen at project creation and cannot be changed afterward; migrate by creating a new
project in the target region.

## Security
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Business plans support SSO via
SAML and SCIM user provisioning. Acme Cloud is SOC 2 Type II certified.

## Support response times
- Starter: community forum only.
- Team: email, responses within 2 business days.
- Business: priority email and chat, responses within 4 business hours.
