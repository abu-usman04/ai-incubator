# Acme Cloud — Employee Handbook

## About Acme Cloud
Acme Cloud is a managed data-platform company founded in 2019, headquartered in Tashkent with
remote teams across Central Asia and Europe. Our mission is to make reliable data infrastructure
accessible to small and mid-sized businesses.

## Working hours and remote work
Standard working hours are 09:00–18:00 local time, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require manager
approval and are reviewed every six months.

## Time off
- Annual paid vacation: 24 working days per year, accruing monthly.
- Sick leave: up to 10 paid days per year; a doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days.
- Public holidays follow the national calendar.
- Unused vacation: up to 5 days may be carried over into the next year; the rest expires on
  December 31.

## Equipment and expenses
New employees receive a laptop and a one-time 1,500,000 UZS home-office stipend. Work-related
expenses are reimbursed within 14 days of submitting a receipt through the finance portal.

## Code of conduct
Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and sharing
of confidential customer data are grounds for immediate disciplinary action. Report concerns to
your manager or to people@acmecloud.example.

## Performance reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each review covers goals, peer feedback, and a
development plan. Promotions and salary adjustments are decided during the December cycle.
